🎓 Install Dr. Claude

Your free study professor. It reads your classes from Canvas and teaches, quizzes, and tests you at your class’s pace. Setup takes about 2 minutes.

1

Copy the install line

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Blondeylocks/dr-claude-updates/main/install-mac.sh | bash

That’s all you copy. It installs Dr. Claude for you, with no password and no admin needed.

2

Open Terminal

Press ⌘ Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Return.

A small window with text opens. That’s Terminal. It’s normal.

3

Paste and press Return

Click in the Terminal window, press ⌘ Cmd + V, then Return.

Wait about 2 minutes. Dr. Claude opens by itself and walks you through the rest. Next time, open it from your Applications folder or the Dr. Claude icon on your Desktop.

Mac asks to let Dr. Claude use a folder? Click Allow. It keeps your class files in Documents.
Stuck? Take a screenshot of the Terminal window and send it to the person who sent you this link.
1

Download Dr. Claude

⬇️ Download for Windows

About 11 MB. It saves to your Downloads folder.

2

Unzip it

Open Downloads, right-click Dr-Claude-Setup, pick Extract All…, then click Extract.

Don’t run it from inside the zip. Extract it first.

3

Double-click “Install Dr. Claude”

In the new Dr Claude Setup folder, double-click Install Dr. Claude. It sets everything up and opens Dr. Claude.

Blue “Windows protected your PC” box? Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows that for new apps that aren’t from a big company.

Next time, open Dr. Claude from the icon on your Desktop or the Start menu.
Stuck? Take a screenshot and send it to the person who sent you this link.
